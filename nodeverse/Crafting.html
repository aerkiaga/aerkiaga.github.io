Crafting


Crafting refers to the ability to produce items by transforming other items.


Instructions

Hand crafting

Open the menu (key 'I' by default), select the Inventory tab and enter the crafting interface by pressing the corresponding button. There is a list of available crafting recipes, each on one row, with the input items on the left and the outputs on the right. If you have enough of the item on your inventory, the numbers below them will be in white, else in red. If you have enough of all items to perform a recipe, you can click on it to do so.
After the recipe is done, the output item will be placed in a special slot; you should open your inventory and move it to the desired location after crafting (unless you're going to immediately craft more of the same item).


Station crafting

Press use (right click by default) while looking at the node that acts as a crafting station. Then follow the instructions as for hand crafting. The recipes will differ. The following stations exist:

  • Furnace
  • Blast furnace
  • Anvil

See their recipes in their respective pages. Hand crafting recipes are documented here (see below).
