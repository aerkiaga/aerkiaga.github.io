Ores


Ores are naturally-occurring precursors to many refined materials. They take the shape of full blocks of mineral, which may be mined to drop the substances they are composed of, as items. Multiple ores may drop the same substance. Each planet only contains a few kinds of ores, making it necessary to travel across them to find new ones.


Surface deposits

Generate as tall spikes made of the mineral, in specific planets.


Drop Sulfur pieces:

  • Sulfur (appears in scorching planets).

Other:

  • Solid ammonia (appears in freezing planets).

Vein ores

Generate as horizontal strata of the mineral, embedded in Stone.


Drop Carbon:

  • Anthracite (frequency: 40, plus it appears in all dead, non-scorching planets).

Drop Iron oxide:

  • Hematite (frequency: 100).
  • Magnetite (frequency: 100).
  • Goethite (frequency: 50).
  • Limonite (frequency: 50).

Drop Aluminium hydroxide:

  • Gibbsite (frequency: 150).
  • Boehmite (frequency: 70).
  • Diaspore (frequency: 70).

Drop Calcium carbonate:

  • Calcite (frequency: 70).
  • Aragonite (frequency: 50).

Drop Sodium chloride:

  • Halite (frequency: 80).

Drop Magnesium hydroxide:

  • Brucite (frequency: 50).

Drop Potassium nitrate:

  • Niter (frequency: 50).

Drop Cobalt arsenate:

  • Erythrite (frequency: 30).
