Planets

Planets are independent spaces that may be navigated, with their own world generation and resources.


Interface

Navigation across planets can be done by flying into space (see Tutorial) and opening the Navigation tab in the menu.



Each planet has an icon of itself in the interface.



Planets also have an entry in the Discoveries tab.



Types

Vacuum

Never contain any Water or other liquids, or life forms. Their surface is covered in craters, and they may have significantly lower gravity than other planets. They are mainly useful for finding Ores.



Freezing

Their surface is covered in Ice and Snow, and possibly Liquid hydrocarbon. They never bear any life forms. Spikes of Solid ammonia may appear.



Cold

Their surface is covered in Snow. May contain Water with Ice patches, or life forms (2/3 of all such planets).



Temperate

May contain Water. Always have living organisms, and in half of them, they are present in great abundance.



Hot

May contain Water, or life forms (2/3 of all such planets).



Scorching

Their surface may contain Lava. They never bear any life forms. Spikes of Sulfur may appear.



Reducing

The rarest type. Most do not have any life forms, but some do, and a few even have an abundance of them.
